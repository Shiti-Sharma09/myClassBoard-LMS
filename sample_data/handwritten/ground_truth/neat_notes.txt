Magnets: Class Notes
- A magnet attracts iron, nickel and cobalt.
- Every magnet has two poles, north and south.
- Like poles repel and unlike poles attract.
- A freely hanging magnet points north and south.
- Wood, plastic and glass are non magnetic.
Uses: compass, fridge door and toys.

States of Water
- Water has three states, solid, liquid and gas.
- Ice melts at 0 degrees Celsius.
- Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.
- Evaporation is faster in sunlight and wind.
- Clouds form when water vapour condenses.
Water cycle: evaporation, condensation, rain.
